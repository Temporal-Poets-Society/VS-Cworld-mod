Cylinder World turns your map into a planet. Travel east or west through an open ocean and come out on the other side, with no visible edge. Explore from the equator to frozen poles, with latitude-based climates, seasons and day length.

I made this mod because I wanted to travel around the world in Vintage Story. It is tailored mainly for use with Earth, which requires it.

Current release: 1.1.2, for Earth 2.0; it keeps Earth servers with many players from stalling once a second. The whole mod was reviewed and fixed for 1.1: smoother crossings, chests and other block changes kept in step on both sides of the seam, safer world generation at the poles, and a feature that a game update breaks now switches off alone instead of crashing. Full documentation is in the wiki.

Features

  • East-west wrapping: players, mounts, boats, drifting items and animals cross between matching ocean edges. The far side is loaded and sent to your game before you arrive, so you land in a world that is already there.
  • Planetary climate: one equator and two poles replace repeating climate bands. High latitudes have long summer days, short winter days and polar day and night; seasons reverse in the southern hemisphere.
  • Frozen poles: tundra gives way to ice sheets, floes and impassable ice walls. Extreme winter cold makes polar exploration dangerous: on the ice sheet a cold exposure meter fills, and a full one kills.
  • Earth's poles: in an Earth world the real Antarctica and Arctic are kept, with seasonal sea ice that freezes in winter and thaws in summer.
  • Climate-based spawn: your starting climate sets your spawn latitude, and vanilla's story locations move with it.

Getting started

  1. Requires Vintage Story 1.22.0 or newer. Install on the server and every client.
  2. Create a new world with world type standard.
  3. Recommended world size: 256k or 512k wide. A world more than twice as wide as it is long gets Earth's proportions (the ring is twice the pole-to-pole distance). Smaller worlds can be used for testing.

Adding the mod to an existing world switches it on, but chunks already generated near the world edges keep their old terrain and spawn stays where it is. Removing the mod from a save is unsupported.

Compatibility and settings

Works with vanilla terrain and Earth. Sea's waves repeat seamlessly around the ring, and World Map charts the planet with latitude and longitude.

Terra Pretty and other terrain or climate overhauls are not yet verified.

Settings are in VintagestoryData/ModConfig/cylinderworld.json: the seam, polar walls, ice and climate. Most world-layout settings are saved when the world is created and only affect new worlds. The sea mist at the seam is off in new worlds; an admin can switch it on with /worldconfig cylinderSeamMist true. Every setting is explained in the wiki.

Commands

  • /cyl info shows your latitude and longitude, the seam, the walls, the temperature and your polar cold exposure.
  • /cyl map saves a picture of the whole ring to VintagestoryData/CylinderWorld/.
  • /cyl goseam, /cyl golat and /cyl gowall teleport for testing (teleport privilege).

Known issues

  • Crossings can cause a short hitch, especially on boats or in multiplayer.
  • The two sides of the seam can differ by a snow layer.
  • The vanilla map does not wrap, and the HUD shows raw X coordinates (/cyl info and World Map show latitude and longitude).

When reporting crossing problems, include the Seam jump lines from your client and server logs.
