Cylinder World turns your map into a planet. Travel east or west through a misty ocean and emerge on the other side. Explore from the equator to frozen poles, with latitude-based climates, seasons and day length.

I made this mod because I wanted to travel around the world in Vintage Story. It is currently tailored mainly for use with Earth Map.

Current release: 1.1.0, the companion of Earth 2.0.0. Feedback and bug reports are welcome. Development is focused on testing, improvements and broader mod compatibility, including terrain generators such as Terra Pretty.

Features

  • East-west wrapping: players, mounts, boats and other entities cross between matching ocean edges. The destination is loaded ahead of your arrival.
  • Planetary climate: one equator and two poles replace repeating climate bands. High latitudes have long summer days, short winter days and polar day/night; seasons reverse in the southern hemisphere.
  • Frozen poles: tundra gives way to ice sheets, floes and impassable ice walls. Extreme winter cold makes polar exploration dangerous.
  • Climate-based spawn: your starting climate determines your spawn latitude, and vanilla story locations move with it.

Getting started

  1. Requires Vintage Story 1.22.0 or newer. Install on the server and every client.
  2. Create a new world with world type standard.
  3. Recommended world size: 256k or 512k. Smaller worlds can be used for testing.

Existing worlds are not recommended: previously generated terrain keeps its old edges. Removing the mod from a save is unsupported.

Compatibility and settings

Works with vanilla terrain and Earth Map. In a new Earth world it automatically keeps Antarctica's terrain and adds seasonal polar sea ice; other worlds get its own ice plateaus ("PolarIceMode" in VintagestoryData/ModConfig/cylinderworld.json: default, earth or plateaus).

Terra Pretty and other terrain or climate overhauls are not yet verified. Compatibility testing and improvements are planned as development continues.

The config also controls the seam, mist, polar walls and climate. Most world-layout settings are saved when the world is created and only affect new worlds.

Commands

  • /cyl info shows coordinates, climate and polar cold exposure.
  • /cyl map saves a world preview to VintagestoryData/CylinderWorld/.

Known issues

  • Crossings can cause a short hitch, especially on boats or in multiplayer.
  • The two sides of the seam can differ by a snow layer.
  • The vanilla map does not wrap, and the HUD shows raw X coordinates (World Map draws the planet and shows latitude and longitude).

When reporting crossing problems, include the Seam jump lines from your client and server logs.
